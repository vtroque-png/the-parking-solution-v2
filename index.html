About The Parking Solution

South Florida parking leadership built on service, accountability, and teamwork.

The Parking Solution (TPS) was founded in 2011 by industry professionals with extensive parking management experience and a shared vision of building a company committed to exceptional customer service, highly trained employees, and long-term financial stability.

With decades of combined experience, TPS leadership has provided parking and transportation services for residential communities, hotels, hospitals, country clubs, restaurants, sporting events, entertainment venues, banquet facilities, and private events.

We view every client relationship as a partnership and work closely with property owners, managers, and stakeholders to improve operations, enhance the customer experience, and deliver consistent service across every property we serve.
